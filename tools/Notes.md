
for your reference when deciding which foods are for which classes

Classes that Receive Mana Increases from Food/Drink
These classes use mana, and therefore benefit from food that restores both health and mana (or water/drinks that restore only mana): 
Mage (All specs)
Priest (All specs)
Shaman (All specs)
Paladin (All specs)
Druid (All specs)
Monk (All specs)
Warlock (Though they often use Life Tap, they still have a mana pool that can be replenished). 
Classes that Do NOT Receive Mana Increases from Food
These classes do not have a mana bar and therefore gain no resource benefit from drinking or eating mana-specific food (though they still benefit from the "Well Fed" buff provided by high-end food): 
Warrior (Rage)
Rogue (Energy/Combo Points)
Hunter (Focus)
Death Knight (Runic Power)
Demon Hunter (Fury/Pain)
